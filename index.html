BotAss

A personal assistant for a single user. Not a public service.

BotAss is a private, self-hosted assistant that its owner runs on his own computer. It helps that one person with his own Gmail and Google Calendar: it can search and read his mail, see his calendar, prepare email drafts and propose calendar events.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the Google Cloud project that BotAss belongs to. There is no sign-up, no account system and no way for anyone else to use it. If you reached a Google sign-in screen for BotAss and you are not its owner, please do not grant access.

What it does with Google data

  • Reads the owner's Gmail messages and calendar events when he asks a question about them.
  • Creates email drafts in the owner's own mailbox.
  • Sends an email or creates/changes a calendar event only after the owner explicitly confirms each action (a separate "Send / Cancel" confirmation for every item).
  • Never deletes mail or calendar events.

Where the data goes

Processing happens locally on the owner's computer. Google data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising or for training models. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
